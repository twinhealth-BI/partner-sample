Engagement Overview

Composite engagement view

Engagement measures how consistently a member is showing up for the programme day-to-day — logging meals, staying active, logging weight, eating green-rated food, keeping up with supplements, and completing daily action-card tasks. The Full Engagement Score is the average of Signals 1–3 (Meal Logging, Steps, Weight Logs); GFY Rate, Overall Action Score and Supplement Adherence are tracked alongside it as supporting signals. All cards below are the single source of truth for these numbers — matching values are not repeated elsewhere on this tab.
